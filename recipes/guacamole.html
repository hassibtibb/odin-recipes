Guacamole

Description

Guacamole is a fresh and creamy Mexican dip made from ripe avocados, lime juice, and a mix of savory ingredients. It's simple to make, naturally healthy, and perfect for serving with tortilla chips or as a topping for tacos and other dishes.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 pinch ground cayenne pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Mash the avocado with a fork, leaving some small chunks for texture.
  3. Stir in the lime juice and salt.
  4. Mix in the onion, cilantro, tomatoes, and garlic.
  5. Sprinkle in the cayenne pepper if using, and stir to combine.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes for the best flavor.
  7. Serve with tortilla chips.

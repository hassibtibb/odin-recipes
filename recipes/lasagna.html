Lasagna

Description

Classic lasagna is a comforting Italian baked pasta dish made of layers of flat pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, and creamy cheese. It's baked in the oven until bubbly and golden on top, making it a perfect meal for family dinners or gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz jar of marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions, then drain.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic over medium heat.
  4. Stir in the marinara sauce, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the parmesan cheese.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat layers.
  8. Top the final layer with remaining mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15 minutes.
  10. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
